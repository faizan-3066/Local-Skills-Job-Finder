About Us

Local Skills & Job Finder is a management system that connects skilled workers with customers in the same city or area. It provides a digital platform where customers can easily find and connect with service providers according to their required skills and location.

The Problem We Solve

Finding the right person for household work is often difficult. Sometimes workers are busy or do not respond, which causes delays. People usually depend on references to find workers. Our platform provides an easier way to find available service providers in local areas.

Our Objectives

Our main objective is to help customers find skilled workers without wasting time. We also aim to help service providers get more work and connect with new customers through an online platform.
